About FoodWaste Connect

Our mission is to reduce food waste and help surplus food reach people and organizations who need it.

Our Mission

FoodWaste Connect connects food donors, NGOs, and volunteers through a single platform. The goal is to make it easier to collect surplus food and redistribute it to communities in need.

How It Works

For Donors

Donors can register and share information about surplus food available for collection. They can manage their donations and track their donation history.

For NGOs

NGOs can find available food donations and request food for their communities. They can manage their food requests through the platform.

For Volunteers

Volunteers help collect food from donors and deliver it to NGOs. They can manage pickup requests and help coordinate food redistribution.

For Administrators

Administrators manage users, donations, NGOs, volunteers, and reports to support the operation of the platform.
