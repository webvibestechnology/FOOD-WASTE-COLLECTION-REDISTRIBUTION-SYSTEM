How FoodWaste Connect Works

🍱

For Donors

Donate your surplus food instead of letting it go to waste. Share available food with organizations that can distribute it to people in need.

🤝

For NGOs

Find available food donations and request food for your community. Help ensure surplus food reaches people who need it.

🚚

For Volunteers

Help collect donated food from donors and deliver it to NGOs. Your time and effort can help reduce food waste in the community.
